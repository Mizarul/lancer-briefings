# Mission // #002

## Legally Distinct Shadowrun

# Goals

- Continue the Investigation.
- Figure out what the angles of the respective factions are.
- What's the deal with the strange monstrosities?
- What happened up north?

# So Far

Our illustrious band of Misfits has established an operating base and ingratiated themselves with the local factions.
During the course of their operations they've found information about the missing agent, some of his involvement with the other factions as well as a previous colleague.

They've also come in contact with strange lifeforms, which have shown strange reaction towards Cacimar, both overtly hostile and reassuring. *Affected by their touch and influence, what will this child of Andernach find?*

Gus has, with help of his temporary colelagues from the docks, basically transformed the bar into a completely new look. It serves as a place of respite and to strengthen bonds within the team and with the factions. *Old ties lie forgotten, but the past is never far, always right behind.*

Victoria has offered helping hands wherever she could, assured the continued survival of her cat Gadget, and is currently in a feud with her her NHP Deidra to not have it sink the city in a massive explosion. *Will she see her passion through, or be found wanting?*

Shinnosuke spearheaded the investigation and, together with Cacimar, managed to make quite the headway into Rhine Labs and their ongoing projects. With a myriad leads and samples, the threads may soon untangle. *When the time comes, will he open the door? Even know it's there?*
